Play Kith on Meta Ray-Ban Display with your phone and iPad friends.

Add Kith to glasses
  1. Open this page on the iPhone paired with your glasses. Enable Developer Mode in the Meta AI app, then add Kith.
  2. Open Kith from the glasses’ app menu. Select Walk while facing forward. Swipe between actions; pinch to select.
  3. For camera quests, keep Kith Camera open on your iPhone. On the glasses choose More → Camera quests, enter its eight-character code on the phone, and start the glasses camera.
